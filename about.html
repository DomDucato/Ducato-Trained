About Me

My name is Dominic Ducato, and I am a collegiate wrestler at Minnesota State University, Mankato. I previously competed at St. Cloud State University and earned NCAA Division II All-American honors.

Wrestling has helped shape who I am through discipline, hard work, consistency, sacrifice, and learning to overcome adversity. The lessons I've learned through wrestling extend far beyond competition.

I created Ducato Trained to share my wrestling knowledge and experiences with the next generation of athletes. My goal is to help every wrestler improve their technique, build confidence, and develop the character and habits necessary for success on and off the mat.

My Coaching Philosophy

My coaching philosophy focuses on helping wrestlers become not only great athletes but also good people. I believe wrestling teaches important lessons such as discipline, perseverance, teamwork, honesty, and accountability.

While I am extremely competitive, I value character development more than winning alone. I want my athletes to develop hard work, integrity, and good sportsmanship because those qualities are important long after their wrestling careers are finished.

Fundamentals First

Every wrestler has different strengths, weaknesses, and goals. I believe in personalized instruction that helps athletes maximize their potential.

Mastering the fundamentals is essential for success. My own achievements came from building a strong technical foundation, and I want to help my athletes develop that same foundation through consistent practice and repetition.

Mental Toughness & Confidence

Wrestling requires physical strength, stamina, and agility, but I believe mental strength is even more important.

I want wrestlers to develop confidence, consistency, and the ability to handle pressure and adversity. My goal is to teach athletes how to remain calm, focused, and determined when challenges arise.

Teamwork & Accountability

Although wrestling is often considered an individual sport, friendships and teamwork are essential. A supportive team environment encourages athletes to hold one another accountable and push each other to reach higher standards.

"You fight harder for your team when they are your best friends."

Growth & Character

I believe in setting realistic, achievable goals and teaching a growth mindset. Challenges and setbacks should be viewed as opportunities to improve.

My ultimate goal is to create a positive, demanding, and enjoyable environment where wrestlers can grow, have fun, and become more confident in themselves. The values they learn should benefit them in their education, relationships, careers, and everyday lives.
