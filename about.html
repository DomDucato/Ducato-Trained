About Me

My name is Dominic Ducato, and I am a collegiate wrestler at Minnesota State University, Mankato. I previously competed at St. Cloud State University, where I earned NCAA Division II All-American honors.

Wrestling has played an important role in shaping who I am today. Throughout my career, I've learned the value of discipline, hard work, consistency, sacrifice, and overcoming adversity.

These lessons extend far beyond competition. I created Ducato Trained to share my wrestling knowledge and experience with the next generation of athletes.

My goal is to help wrestlers improve their technique, develop confidence, and become stronger individuals both on and off the mat.

Wrestling Accomplishments

Coaching Experience

In addition to my collegiate wrestling experience, I have worked with youth and high school wrestlers to help develop their skills and confidence.

My coaching experience includes Pursuit Wrestling Club in Rice, Minnesota, along with coaching youth and high school wrestlers in the St. Cloud area.

I enjoy working with athletes of different ages and skill levels. My goal is to create a positive, challenging, and supportive environment where wrestlers can learn and continue improving.

My Coaching Philosophy

My coaching philosophy focuses on helping wrestlers become not only great athletes but also good people.

I believe wrestling teaches important life lessons such as discipline, perseverance, teamwork, honesty, and accountability.

While I am highly competitive, I value character development more than winning alone.

Fundamentals First

Every wrestler is different, with unique strengths, weaknesses, and goals.

I believe mastering the fundamentals is essential for long-term wrestling success. My own accomplishments came from developing a strong technical foundation through consistency and repetition.

Mental Toughness & Confidence

Wrestling requires both physical and mental toughness.

My goal is to help athletes develop confidence, consistency, and the ability to handle pressure and adversity.

I want wrestlers to learn how to remain calm, focused, and determined when facing difficult challenges.

Teamwork & Accountability

Although wrestling is often viewed as an individual sport, I believe friendships and teamwork are essential to long-term success.

A positive team culture encourages wrestlers to support one another, hold each other accountable, and push each other toward higher standards.

"You fight harder for your team when they are your best friends."

Growth Mindset & Character

I believe in setting realistic, achievable goals while encouraging a growth mindset.

Obstacles and setbacks should be viewed as opportunities to learn and improve.

My ultimate goal is to create a positive, challenging, and enjoyable training environment where athletes can grow, learn, and have fun while working toward their goals.

I want every wrestler I coach to develop skills and character traits that will help them throughout their education, relationships, careers, and everyday lives.
