About Me

My name is Dominic Ducato, and I am a collegiate wrestler at Minnesota State University, Mankato. I previously competed at St. Cloud State University and earned NCAA Division II All-American honors.

Wrestling has played an important role in shaping who I am today. Throughout my career, I've learned the value of discipline, hard work, consistency, sacrifice, and overcoming adversity. These lessons extend far beyond competition.

I created Ducato Trained to share my wrestling knowledge and experiences with the next generation of athletes. My goal is to help wrestlers improve their technique, develop confidence, and become stronger individuals both on and off the mat.

My Coaching Philosophy

My coaching philosophy focuses on helping wrestlers become not only great athletes but also good people. I believe wrestling teaches important life lessons such as discipline, perseverance, teamwork, honesty, and accountability.

While I am highly competitive, I value character development more than winning alone. I want my athletes to develop integrity, a strong work ethic, and good sportsmanship because these qualities are essential to success on and off the mat.

Fundamentals First

Every wrestler is different, with unique strengths, weaknesses, and goals. I am committed to providing personalized instruction that helps each athlete reach their full potential.

I believe mastering the fundamentals is essential for wrestling success. My own accomplishments came from developing a strong technical foundation, and I want to help athletes build that same foundation through consistency and repetition.

Mental Toughness & Confidence

Wrestling requires both physical and mental toughness. While strength, stamina, and agility are important, I believe mental strength is even more essential.

My goal is to help athletes develop confidence, consistency, and the ability to handle pressure and adversity. I want wrestlers to learn how to stay calm, focused, and determined when facing difficult challenges.

Teamwork & Accountability

Although wrestling is often viewed as an individual sport, I believe friendships and teamwork are essential to long-term success.

A positive team culture encourages wrestlers to support one another, hold each other accountable, and push each other toward higher standards.

"You fight harder for your team when they are your best friends."

Growth Mindset & Character

I believe in setting realistic and achievable goals while encouraging a growth mindset. Obstacles and setbacks should be viewed as opportunities to learn and improve.

My ultimate goal is to create a positive, challenging, and enjoyable training environment where athletes can grow, learn, and have fun while working toward their goals.

I want every wrestler I coach to develop valuable skills and character traits that will help them throughout their education, relationships, careers, and everyday lives.
