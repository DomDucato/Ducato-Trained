POLICIES & FAQ

Important information about Ducato Trained wrestling lessons, payments, cancellations, required waivers, and frequently asked questions.

What You Need to Know

At Ducato Trained, my goal is to provide a positive, professional, and organized training experience for wrestlers and their families. Please review the policies below before scheduling a lesson.

Wrestling Lesson Policies

48-Hour Cancellation & Rescheduling Policy

At Ducato Trained, I value your time and appreciate the same consideration in return.

  • 48-Hour Notice: Please provide at least 48 hours' notice if you need to cancel or reschedule a wrestling lesson.
  • Late Cancellation Fee: Cancellations made with less than 48 hours' notice may be subject to a $25 fee.
  • No-Shows: Missed appointments without prior notice may also be subject to a $25 fee.
  • Emergencies: Emergencies and unexpected circumstances will be considered individually.
  • Coach Cancellations: If I need to cancel a lesson, families will have the opportunity to reschedule without an additional charge.

Thank you for respecting my time and helping Ducato Trained provide a positive training experience for every athlete.

Payment Policy

Payment is required before each wrestling lesson begins.

Ducato Trained accepts the following payment methods:

  • Cash: Payment can be made in person before the lesson starts.
  • Venmo: Payment can be sent electronically before the scheduled lesson.
  • Zelle: Payment can be sent electronically before the scheduled lesson.

Venmo and Zelle payment instructions will be provided after lesson arrangements are confirmed.

Any additional travel or facility costs will be discussed and agreed upon before the lesson.

Training Locations

Mankato, Minnesota

Wrestling lessons may take place at the Taylor Center Wrestling Room, Minnesota State University, Mankato, during the academic year, subject to facility availability.

Required Taylor Center Waiver

All wrestlers participating in private lessons at the Taylor Center Wrestling Room must have Minnesota State University's official waiver completed before participating.

For wrestlers under 18, a parent or legal guardian must complete and sign the university's waiver.

The waiver addresses risks associated with wrestling, including the possibility of injury.

No wrestler will be permitted to participate in a Taylor Center lesson without the completed waiver.

The official university waiver and completion instructions will be provided before the scheduled lesson.

Carpentersville, Illinois Area

Summer lessons may be available in Carpentersville and nearby communities. Exact training facilities will be arranged individually.

At-Home Lessons

At-home lessons may be offered depending on travel distance, suitable training space, scheduling, and coach approval.

All lesson locations must be confirmed before the session.

Parent & Guardian Requirements

Ducato Trained provides wrestling instruction for boys and girls in grades K–12.

  • A parent or legal guardian must arrange lessons for wrestlers under 18.
  • Parents or guardians must provide accurate contact information.
  • A parent or guardian must be present for at-home lessons involving minors.
  • For Taylor Center lessons, a parent or legal guardian must sign Minnesota State University's required waiver before their child participates.
  • Families should communicate relevant training restrictions or accommodations privately before the lesson.

Safety & Athlete Conduct

Ducato Trained is committed to creating a positive, respectful, and safe training environment.

  • Wrestlers are expected to demonstrate respect and good sportsmanship.
  • Athletes should arrive prepared and ready to train.
  • Wrestlers should wear appropriate athletic clothing and wrestling shoes.
  • Athletes should follow coaching instructions and facility rules.
  • Training may be adjusted or stopped when necessary for athlete safety.

Frequently Asked Questions

Here are answers to common questions about Ducato Trained wrestling lessons. Click a question to see the answer.

What ages and grades do you coach?

Ducato Trained offers wrestling instruction for boys and girls in kindergarten through 12th grade (K–12).

Do you work with beginners?

Yes! I work with wrestlers of different experience levels, from beginners learning the basics to experienced competitors looking to improve their skills.

Where are lessons held?

During the school year, lessons may take place at the Taylor Center Wrestling Room in Mankato, Minnesota.

During the summer, lessons may be offered near Carpentersville, Illinois. Specific facilities will be confirmed individually.

Is a waiver required for Taylor Center lessons?

Yes. Minnesota State University's official waiver must be completed before a wrestler participates in a lesson at the Taylor Center Wrestling Room.

For wrestlers under 18, the waiver must be completed and signed by a parent or legal guardian.

Instructions will be provided before the lesson.

Do you travel to wrestlers' homes?

At-home lessons may be available depending on driving distance, appropriate training space, scheduling, and coach approval.

A parent or guardian must be present for at-home lessons involving minors.

What should my wrestler bring?

Wrestlers should bring wrestling shoes, comfortable athletic clothing, and water.

Any additional equipment needs will be discussed before the lesson.

How much do lessons cost?

One-on-one lessons: $80 for 60 minutes.

Partner lessons: $100 total for 60 minutes ($50 per wrestler).

Small-group lessons: $40 per wrestler for 90 minutes, with 4–8 wrestlers.

How can I pay for a lesson?

Ducato Trained accepts cash, Venmo, and Zelle. Payment is due before the lesson begins.

How do I schedule a lesson?

Online booking is coming soon. Once available, parents will be able to view available dates and times and request lessons directly through the website.

For now, use the Contact page for questions and inquiries.

Can we choose what techniques to work on?

Absolutely! Lessons can focus on specific techniques, positions, or areas where your wrestler wants to improve.

What if I need to cancel or reschedule?

Please provide at least 48 hours' notice when canceling or rescheduling.

Cancellations with less than 48 hours' notice may be subject to a $25 fee.

Emergencies and special circumstances will be considered individually.
