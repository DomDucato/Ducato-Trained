Welcome to Ducato Trained

Founded by collegiate wrestler and NCAA Division II All-American Dominic Ducato, Ducato Trained focuses on developing fundamentally sound, disciplined, and confident wrestlers. We believe success means more than winning matches. Our training emphasizes technique, mental toughness, hard work, accountability, and character that lasts a lifetime.
