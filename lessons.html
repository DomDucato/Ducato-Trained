Personalized Coaching

I believe the best way to improve in wrestling is through consistent training, strong fundamentals, and attention to detail.

My lessons focus on helping athletes understand techniques, practice them correctly, and gain confidence using them in competition.

Whether we're working on takedowns, positioning, defense, or situational wrestling, I want every athlete to leave training better than when they arrived.

Just as importantly, I emphasize discipline, mental toughness, accountability, and sportsmanship.

Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about Ducato Trained wrestling lessons? Here are answers to some of the most common questions.

What ages and grades do you coach?

Ducato Trained offers wrestling instruction for boys and girls in kindergarten through 12th grade (K–12).

Do you work with beginners?

Yes! I work with wrestlers of different experience levels, from beginners learning the basics to experienced competitors looking to improve their technique.

Where do wrestling lessons take place?

During the school year, lessons can take place at the Taylor Center Wrestling Room at Minnesota State University, Mankato, depending on facility availability.

During the summer, lessons may be offered in Carpentersville, Illinois, and surrounding areas. Specific locations will be arranged individually.

Do you travel to wrestlers' homes?

At-home lessons may be available depending on the location, driving distance, and whether an appropriate training space is available.

All at-home lessons require coach approval and appropriate parent or guardian involvement.

What should my wrestler bring?

Wrestlers should bring wrestling shoes, comfortable athletic clothing, and water.

Any additional equipment requirements will be discussed before the lesson.

How much do lessons cost?

One-on-one lessons cost $80 for 60 minutes.

Partner lessons cost $100 total for two wrestlers for 60 minutes.

Small-group lessons cost $40 per wrestler for 90 minutes, with 4–8 wrestlers.

Any additional facility or travel charges will be discussed beforehand.

How do I schedule a lesson?

Online booking is coming soon! When it becomes available, parents will be able to see available dates and times and request lessons directly through the website.

For now, please use the Contact page to ask questions or inquire about training.

Can I choose what techniques we work on?

Absolutely. Lessons can focus on the specific techniques, positions, or areas where your wrestler wants to improve.

We can also work together to identify areas for technical development.
