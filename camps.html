WRESTLING CAMPS & CLINICS

Build Your Fundamentals. Develop Mental Toughness. Train With Purpose.

More Than Just Wrestling

Ducato Trained focuses on developing skilled wrestlers while building confidence, discipline, teamwork, and character. Coach Dominic Ducato emphasizes fundamentals, technical instruction, consistent effort, and valuable life lessons that extend beyond the mat.

What Athletes Will Learn

Wrestling Fundamentals

Stance, motion, positioning, essential techniques, and repetition to build a strong foundation.

Offense & Defense

Improve takedowns, finishes, defensive reactions, and situational wrestling skills.

Mental Toughness

Develop confidence, composure, discipline, consistency, and the ability to compete under pressure.

Teamwork & Character

Develop accountability, perseverance, sportsmanship, and strong relationships with other wrestlers.
