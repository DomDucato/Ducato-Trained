WRESTLING CAMPS & CLINICS

Build Your Fundamentals. Develop Mental Toughness. Train With Purpose.

More Than Just Wrestling

At Ducato Wrestling, camps and clinics are focused on developing fundamentally sound wrestlers while building confidence, discipline, teamwork, and character. Coach Dominic Ducato emphasizes technical instruction, consistent effort, and learning lessons that extend beyond the mat.

What Athletes Will Learn

Fundamentals

Stance, motion, positioning, essential techniques, and repetition. Develop a strong foundation for success.

Offense & Defense

Improve attacks, finishes, defensive reactions, and situational wrestling.

Mental Toughness

Build confidence, composure, discipline, and the ability to perform under pressure.

Teamwork & Character

Develop accountability, sportsmanship, perseverance, and relationships that make teammates better.
