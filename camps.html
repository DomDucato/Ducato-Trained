WRESTLING CAMPS & CLINICS

Build Your Fundamentals. Develop Mental Toughness. Train With Purpose.

More Than Just Wrestling

At Ducato Trained, wrestling camps and clinics are about more than learning new techniques. Coach Dominic Ducato focuses on developing fundamentally sound wrestlers while building confidence, discipline, teamwork, and character. Every training opportunity emphasizes hard work, technical development, and the life lessons that wrestling teaches both on and off the mat.

What Athletes Will Learn

Wrestling Fundamentals

Develop a strong foundation through stance, motion, positioning, essential wrestling techniques, and consistent repetition.

Offense & Defense

Improve takedowns, finishes, defensive reactions, and situational wrestling skills that translate to competition.

Mental Toughness

Build confidence, discipline, composure, and the ability to remain focused and determined when facing difficult situations.

Teamwork & Character

Develop accountability, perseverance, sportsmanship, and strong relationships with teammates and training partners.
